[JAMIE]
Now explain the PR workflow.

[ALEX]
The workflow is straightforward. First, you open a challenge issue and create a branch from it. Then you make your edits, commit them, and open a pull request. After that, the automation bot checks your PR and leaves feedback. If review is part of the challenge, a peer reads your work and comments on it too. Then you update the PR, respond to feedback, and merge when it is ready.

[ALEX]
The key idea is that each step gives you evidence. Issue, branch, PR, bot feedback, peer comments, merge. That sequence is how you know you are on track.

[JAMIE]
Where does the bot fit in?

[ALEX]
The automated bot is your first reviewer. It checks the pull request quickly and leaves feedback in the PR conversation. It is there to teach, not to punish. If the bot flags something confusing or unexpected, that is a cue to read carefully and ask for help if needed.

[ALEX]
Then peer review adds a human voice. A classmate reads your PR, comments on the changes, and helps you see your work from another contributor’s perspective.

What is the best way to review a classmate’s PR?
