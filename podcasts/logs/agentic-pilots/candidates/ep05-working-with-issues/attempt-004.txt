Cross-Referencing Issues. What does that mean?

[ALEX]
Closing keywords in PR descriptions or issue comments: write "fixes #42" or "closes #150" and when your PR merges, it automatically closes that linked issue. The system recognizes these: closes, closed by, fix, fixes, fixed by, resolve, resolves, resolved by.

[ALEX]
Mentioning another issue in a comment: type #42 anywhere and it becomes a link. The issue timeline shows that the reference was made. This creates a visible trail between related work.

[ALEX]
Cross-repo references: #owner/repo#42 links to issue 42 in a different repository. Useful when work spans multiple projects.
