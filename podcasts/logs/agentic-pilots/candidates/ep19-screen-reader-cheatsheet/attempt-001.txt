Now for Screen Reader Mode Basics. The key idea is that web screen readers usually work in two modes. Browse Mode is for reading and navigating. Focus Mode is for typing and interacting.

[JAMIE]
So the learner should hear that as a switch in responsibility. In one mode, the screen reader is steering. In the other, the page is.

[ALEX]
Right. Browse Mode, also called Virtual Cursor or Reading Mode, is where single-key navigation works. Pressing a letter can jump to the next matching element instead of typing it.

[ALEX]
Focus Mode, also called Forms or Application Mode, is where your keys go straight to the page. That is what you want for text fields, buttons, menus, and other interactive controls.
